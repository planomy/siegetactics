How to Play — custom illustration slots
======================================

Drop PNG or JPG files here using these exact names. Each appears on the
LEFT of that step in the How to Play guide (explanation on the right).

Recommended size: 800×600 or 1024×768, transparent or dark background.
Landscape works best. Until these exist, the game uses fallback art.

  01-mission-loop.png       — HQ → training → siege → results loop
  02-training-modules.png — six module cards / drill screen
  03-forge-xp-coins.png   — Forge XP (permanent) vs siege coins (per fight)
  04-deployment-readiness.png — hero banner + prep checklist / readiness bar
  05-level-shields.png    — three shields under a module card
  06-nav-chart.png        — 2×3 shrouded nav chart at bottom of HQ
  07-armory.png           — Armory / turret unlock screen
  08-siege-field.png      — lawn field with turrets and aliens
  09-nuke-cache.png       — cupcake nuke cache / Granny nuke
  10-results.png          — results screen with stars and stats

Optional: square crops also work — they are letterboxed in the panel.
